Real-estate terminology guide

Glossary of real-estate terms

This glossary explains real-estate terms and the terms used for brokerage, requests and documents, so you know exactly what is meant when they are used.

  1. 01 Property & transactions Terms that describe a property, its condition and the possible purposes of a transaction.
  2. 02 Brokerage & proof of capacity Terms for real-estate brokerage, proving the applicant’s capacity, and the limits of an agreement.
  3. 03 Requests & documents Terms for submitting a request, reviewing information, and the documents that may be shown to you depending on the request’s status.
  4. 04 Information & data review Terms for general information and the available data that the team reviews case by case.
  5. 05 Regulatory reference Terms tied to the official reference for the real-estate sector in the Kingdom.
