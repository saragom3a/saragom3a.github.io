About Sorouh Al-Maali

Deals built on trust

A licensed Saudi real-estate company: we review every request in person before any step, and connect property owners with developers and investors across the Kingdom, never showing your property’s details without your consent.

Sorouh Al-Maali Real Estate is a real-estate company. Its online service lets you send requests and organises the information you provide for review. Sending a request does not release documents or mean the request is accepted.

Our vision

A digital environment that helps organise requests and property information, while the decision stays with those it belongs to.

Our mission

To organise requests for properties and partnerships, and the information submitted about them, within the company’s scope of business.

Our approach

The team considers each request on its purpose and the information provided with it.
