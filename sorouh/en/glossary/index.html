Real-estate terminology guide

Glossary of real-estate terms

This glossary explains real-estate terms and the terms used for brokerage, requests and documents, so you know exactly what is meant when they are used.

The regulatory definitions given here are based on the Real Estate General Authority’s reference; the other definitions explain how each term is used within the Sorouh platform.

01

Property & transactions

Terms that describe a property, its condition and the possible purposes of a transaction.

01

Property

العقار

The land or building that is the subject of a request or transaction.

At Sorouh: Mentioning a property in a request does not mean it is being offered or made available to the public.

Source: Sorouh Platform

# Direct link to this term: Property
02

Raw land

أرض خام

Undeveloped land with no existing building on it, according to the information provided.

At Sorouh: Built-up areas are only stated where they apply to the property.

Source: Sorouh Platform

# Direct link to this term: Raw land
03

Land within a subdivision plan

أرض ضمن مخطط

Land located within a plan, according to the information and documents provided.

At Sorouh: Used to describe where the land is; it does not indicate that the land is being offered or developed.

Source: Sorouh Platform

# Direct link to this term: Land within a subdivision plan
04

Land area

مساحة الأرض

The area of the land in square metres, according to the information or documents provided.

At Sorouh: Used to describe the property; it is not a substitute for checking the relevant documents.

Source: Sorouh Platform

# Direct link to this term: Land area
05

Built-up area

مسطحات البناء

The total area of the built parts of the property.

At Sorouh: Stated for existing buildings, and kept separate from the land area.

Source: Sorouh Platform

# Direct link to this term: Built-up area
06

Occupancy status

حالة الإشغال

Whether the property is vacant or occupied, wholly or partly.

At Sorouh: Shows the occupancy position at the time the information was entered.

Source: Sorouh Platform

Related reading: How to read the net yield on commercial assets without being misled by the numbers

# Direct link to this term: Occupancy status
07

Real-estate transaction

الصفقة العقارية

The sale, purchase or lease of a property or of its usufruct (the right to use it).

At Sorouh: Stating a purpose in a request does not mean a deal has been completed or that any party is bound.

Source: Real Estate General Authority

# Direct link to this term: Real-estate transaction
08

Acquisition

التملّك

A purpose indicating the wish to buy a property or a share in it.

At Sorouh: Submitting a request does not mean the acquisition has been completed or that any party is committed.

Source: Sorouh Platform

# Direct link to this term: Acquisition
09

Development partnership

الشراكة التطويرية

A proposed collaboration between parties to develop a property or a real-estate project.

At Sorouh: This description does not create a partnership or an obligation.

Source: Sorouh Platform

Related reading: Structuring the landowner–developer partnership: where it usually fails

# Direct link to this term: Development partnership
02

Brokerage & proof of capacity

Terms for real-estate brokerage, proving the applicant’s capacity, and the limits of an agreement.

10

Real-estate brokerage

الوساطة العقارية

Mediating between the two parties to complete a real-estate transaction, in return for a commission.

At Sorouh: Used when referring to a brokerage request; submitting the request does not mean the brokerage has been accepted.

Source: Real Estate General Authority

# Direct link to this term: Real-estate brokerage
11

Real-estate broker

الوسيط العقاري

A natural or legal person licensed to practise real-estate brokerage or to provide real-estate services in accordance with the Law.

At Sorouh: The scope of brokerage practice is set by the licence.

Source: Real Estate General Authority

# Direct link to this term: Real-estate broker
12

Brokerage contract

عقد الوساطة

An agreement between the real-estate broker and the beneficiary to complete a real-estate transaction or provide a real-estate service.

At Sorouh: A contract does not come into being merely because a request is submitted.

Source: Real Estate General Authority

# Direct link to this term: Brokerage contract
13

Proof of capacity

إثبات الصفة

A statement or document showing the applicant’s capacity and their connection to the property, where needed.

At Sorouh: May be requested when the applicant’s capacity needs to be verified.

Source: Sorouh Platform

# Direct link to this term: Proof of capacity
14

Applicant’s capacity

صفة مقدم الطلب

The capacity in which a person submits a request, such as owner, attorney or authorised representative.

At Sorouh: A document proving that capacity may be requested, depending on the case.

Source: Sorouh Platform

# Direct link to this term: Applicant’s capacity
15

Purpose of a brokerage request

غرض طلب الوساطة

The purpose the applicant specifies, such as sale, purchase or lease.

At Sorouh: Helps the team understand the request; it does not mean the procedure has been completed.

Source: Sorouh Platform

# Direct link to this term: Purpose of a brokerage request
16

Exclusive brokerage

الوساطة الحصرية

An arrangement in which the brokerage contract gives a single broker the scope of work for the property.

At Sorouh: This arrangement does not come into being merely because a request is submitted.

Source: Sorouh Platform

# Direct link to this term: Exclusive brokerage
03

Requests & documents

Terms for submitting a request, reviewing information, and the documents that may be shown to you depending on the request’s status.

17

Submitting a request

إرسال طلب

Sending information about a property or a transaction purpose so that it can be reviewed.

At Sorouh: Submitting does not mean the request has been accepted or that an agreement has been concluded.

Source: Sorouh Platform

# Direct link to this term: Submitting a request
18

Reference number

الرقم المرجعي

A number that identifies a request when it is followed up.

At Sorouh: The number alone confers no additional rights.

Source: Sorouh Platform

# Direct link to this term: Reference number
19

Data review

مراجعة البيانات

Reviewing the information and documents provided to determine what is needed to complete the request.

At Sorouh: A review does not mean the request has been accepted or approved.

Source: Sorouh Platform

# Direct link to this term: Data review
20

Completing the information

استكمال البيانات

Adding information or documents that the reviewer asks for, where needed.

At Sorouh: What is required varies from case to case.

Source: Sorouh Platform

# Direct link to this term: Completing the information
21

Documents available to you

المستندات المتاحة لك

The documents the Sorouh team shares with you according to the status of your request.

At Sorouh: Documents are not made available merely because a request is submitted.

Source: Sorouh Platform

# Direct link to this term: Documents available to you
22

Request follow-up

متابعة الطلب

Viewing the status of a request and what is needed to complete it.

At Sorouh: Follow-up is done by sending your reference number to the Sorouh team by WhatsApp or email.

Source: Sorouh Platform

# Direct link to this term: Request follow-up
04

Information & data review

Terms for general information and the available data that the team reviews case by case.

23

Potential uses of a property

استخدامات محتملة للعقار

Uses that can be explored for a property in light of the information available about it.

At Sorouh: They do not determine the use that is legally permitted.

Source: Sorouh Platform

Related reading: Why highest and best use must come before any development decision

# Direct link to this term: Potential uses of a property
24

Preliminary indicators

مؤشرات أولية

General, preliminary information that helps in understanding a property or the market.

At Sorouh: They are not a valuation or a recommendation.

Source: Sorouh Platform

# Direct link to this term: Preliminary indicators
25

Real-estate feasibility study

دراسة جدوى عقارية

An analysis that estimates the feasibility of a real-estate project or decision across its main aspects, such as market, cost, return and risk.

At Sorouh: The appearance of this term does not mean that a study or recommendation is being provided.

Source: Sorouh Platform

Related reading: Why highest and best use must come before any development decision

# Direct link to this term: Real-estate feasibility study
26

Real-estate market indicator

مؤشر السوق العقاري

A statistical measure describing a movement or change in the market over a defined period and scope.

At Sorouh: To be read in light of its source, period and scope.

Source: Sorouh Platform

# Direct link to this term: Real-estate market indicator
27

Available data

البيانات المتاحة

The information and documents provided with a request, or available for review, depending on the case.

At Sorouh: They may need to be completed before they are reviewed.

Source: Sorouh Platform

# Direct link to this term: Available data
05

Regulatory reference

Terms tied to the official reference for the real-estate sector in the Kingdom.

28

Real-estate register

السجل العقاري

A set of documents setting out a property’s description, location and status, and the rights and obligations attached to it.

At Sorouh: Request information does not replace the real-estate register documents.

Source: Real Estate General Authority

# Direct link to this term: Real-estate register
29

Title deed

صك تسجيل الملكية

An ownership document issued by the real-estate register as evidence of the property’s details, including its type, status and owner, and the rights, restrictions and obligations attached to it.

At Sorouh: Request information and attached documents are not a substitute for the title deed.

Source: Real Estate General Authority

# Direct link to this term: Title deed
30

In-rem property registration

التسجيل العيني للعقار

A system in which the property that is the subject of a real right is the basis for recording that right in the real-estate register.

At Sorouh: The appearance of this term does not mean the property is registered in rem.

Source: Real Estate General Authority

# Direct link to this term: In-rem property registration
31

FAL licence

رخصة فال

An official document authorising individuals and establishments to practise the real-estate activity specified in the licence.

At Sorouh: The permitted activity is the one stated in the licence.

Source: Real Estate General Authority

# Direct link to this term: FAL licence
32

Wafi: off-plan sale and lease of real-estate projects

بيع وتأجير مشروعات عقارية على الخارطة «وافي»

A regulation governing the sale and lease of real-estate projects off-plan; its platform provides the qualification and licences needed by those who wish to practise this activity.

At Sorouh: The appearance of this term, or of project information, does not mean the project is licensed or offered for off-plan sale or lease.

Source: Wafi

# Direct link to this term: Wafi: off-plan sale and lease of real-estate projects
33

Ejar

إيجار

An integrated electronic network that regulates the real-estate rental sector and protects the rights of landlords, tenants and real-estate brokers.

At Sorouh: A mention of Ejar does not mean a contract is registered or authenticated, or that the platform is connected to Ejar; a status is shown only once it has been verified.

Source: Ejar

# Direct link to this term: Ejar
34

Subdivision of real-estate units

فرز الوحدات العقارية

A procedure governing the subdivision or re-subdivision of a building or real-estate complex into several real-estate units, setting out the rights and duties of each unit.

At Sorouh: Used for buildings and complexes or their units; the expression «فرز الأصول» (“asset subdivision”) is not used as a general regulatory substitute.

Source: Real Estate General Authority

# Direct link to this term: Subdivision of real-estate units
35

Real-estate advertising licence

ترخيص الإعلان العقاري

An authorisation issued by the Authority for visual, print or audio advertising of the disposal of a property by any means.

At Sorouh: The appearance of this term does not mean that a real-estate advertisement exists or has been published.

Source: Real Estate General Authority

# Direct link to this term: Real-estate advertising licence
