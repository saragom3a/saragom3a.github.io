01

Nature of the link

The document-sharing link is private, temporary and intended solely for the invited party. It does not constitute a public offer, nor an authorisation, a power of attorney, proof of ownership, or a commitment to complete a transaction.

02

Scope of use

The content is to be used solely for the purpose of reviewing the property that is the subject of the link. The recipient undertakes not to re-send, copy or publish the documents, or to use them for any purpose that has not been agreed.

03

Sensitive documents and data

The link does not give access to title deeds, identity documents, powers of attorney or documents that prove ownership. Attachments are limited to what has been made available through the link. Any request for a sensitive document is handled according to the established procedure.

04

Confidentiality and protection of rights

Photographs, plans and sketches may be stamped with Sorouh’s identity (branding) or a reference number. These marks may not be removed or altered, and ownership data or a precise location may not be inferred from them, outside the authorised purpose.

05

Revocation and monitoring

The Sorouh team may revoke the link or terminate access at any time. Acceptance of the terms and the time of use are recorded to the extent necessary to protect the link and review its use, without requesting additional personal data from the viewer.

06

No reliance

The materials shown are preliminary and indicative, and are not a substitute for due diligence or for independent legal, engineering and financial verification. Nor do they constitute a guarantee of the soundness of the address, the area, the regulatory status or the feasibility.
