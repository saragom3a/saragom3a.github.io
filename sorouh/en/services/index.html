Fee details and payment terms +

Notice on fees and scope of service

  1. 01The brackets are indicative for institutional investment studies; the scope and final value are set in a written proposal according to the asset’s complexity and the deliverables required.
  2. 02The commission is paid by the party that contracts with the broker, unless otherwise agreed in writing (Article 14/2).
  3. 03The commission is due when the deal closes while the contract is in force, or within two months of its expiry on proof of brokerage (Article 15).
  4. 04A deposit may not exceed 5% of the transaction value, and the broker may not hold it as security for their fee (Article 13).

Where a brokerage request applies, any brokerage service requires a separate agreement and verification of the scope of service and licensing before it starts.
