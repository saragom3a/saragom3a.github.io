Before you begin

Frequently asked questions

Every case starts from the information you have; the Sorouh team then works out what its review needs.

Will my property’s details be made public?+

No. Your request is reviewed internally, and nothing from it is shared except according to permission and the request’s status, and with your consent.

Does sending a request mean it is accepted?+

Sending a request does not mean acceptance, disclosure or any automatic action; the next step depends on the request’s status and the information available.

What documents do I need to start?+

To start, whatever basic details you have are enough. After the review the team tells you what is needed to complete the request, and you can send documents in the same WhatsApp chat.

How do I follow up on my request?+

Every request carries a reference number shown to you before you send it. Keep it and send it to the team by WhatsApp or email to ask about your request.

What are the fees?+

The indicative fee and commission brackets and the study fee calculator are on the services page.
