Glossary

Brokerage and proof of capacity

The terms of real-estate brokerage, of proving the applicant’s capacity, and the limits of the agreement.

Glossary
Brokerage terms (6)
  1. 01

    Real-estate brokerage

    Source: Real Estate General Authority (REGA)

    Mediating the completion of a real-estate transaction between its two parties in return for a commission.

    At Sorouh: Used when referring to a brokerage request; sending a request does not mean the brokerage is accepted.

  2. 02

    Real-estate broker

    Source: Real Estate General Authority (REGA)

    A natural or legal person licensed to practise real-estate brokerage or provide real-estate services under the law.

    At Sorouh: What a broker may do is set by the licence and its scope.

  3. 03

    Brokerage contract

    Source: Real Estate General Authority (REGA)

    An agreement between the broker and the client to complete a real-estate transaction or provide a real-estate service.

    At Sorouh: Sending a request does not by itself create a contract.

  4. 04

    FAL licence

    Source: Real Estate General Authority (REGA)

    An official document authorising individuals and businesses to practise the real-estate activity named in the licence.

    At Sorouh: The permitted activity is the one stated in the licence.

  5. 05

    Proof of capacity

    Source: Sorouh Platform

    A statement or document showing the applicant’s capacity and connection to the property, where needed.

    At Sorouh: It may be requested when the capacity needs to be verified.

  6. 06

    Exclusive brokerage

    Source: Sorouh Platform

    An arrangement in which the brokerage contract gives a single broker the mandate for the property.

    At Sorouh: Sending a request does not by itself create this arrangement.
