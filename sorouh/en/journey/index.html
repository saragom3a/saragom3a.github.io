The Sorouh path

Define your request, then follow its progress

Start by identifying the property, the land or the purpose of your request, then follow its status and anything that may be needed to complete it, depending on your capacity.

Choose what suits your request

Choose the request that fits your purpose

Each type of request has its own basic information, depending on your capacity and the company’s scope of business.

  1. 01

    I have a property or a usufruct right

    Send the details of a commercial or office building, a hotel, commercial land or a usufruct right. The team reviews the request and the information provided before considering sharing anything with another party.

    Send your property or land details
  2. 02

    I am looking for a property or a partnership

    State the purpose of your request and the category of property you need, and add any information that helps us review it.

    Send your request Available opportunities
  3. 03

    I want to request real-estate brokerage

    Send a real-estate brokerage request for a property or a project. The team reviews it before deciding whether it can proceed; sending it does not mean the brokerage is accepted or the property is put on the market.

    Send a brokerage request
  4. 04

    I represent a company or an investment portfolio

    Submit the request in the entity’s name, stating its purpose and your capacity. The information is reviewed, and any further requirements are set out.

    Send a request for an entity

A clear step

From receipt to the right action.

You send your request as a structured message with a reference number, by WhatsApp or email, and the Sorouh team follows up with you on the same channel.

  1. 01

    Send the request

    You share the basic information you have about the property or your area of interest.

  2. 02

    Human review

    The team reviews the information available and works out what is needed to complete the picture.

  3. 03

    The available step

    The status, or what is needed next, is set out within the limits of permission and the request’s scope.
