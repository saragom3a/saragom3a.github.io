Governance & privacy

Privacy policy

This page explains how client data and property documents are handled, within the limits necessary to process requests and protect confidentiality.

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Notice: this policy may be revised when regulatory obligations or the platform’s services change, and the version in force is published on this page.

01

Scope of the policy

This policy explains how Sorouh Al-Maali Real Estate Company handles the data submitted through the platform, including contact details, property data, location, photographs, plans, sketches (kroki) and documents. The policy applies to the property-data submission forms, the document-sharing links and the related requests.

02

Sending requests through this website

This website does not send form data to the company’s servers and does not upload any files. A draft of your request is kept in your browser on this device only, for up to 30 days, and is deleted when you start a new request. The request reaches the company only when you send it yourself by WhatsApp or email; those services are subject to their providers’ own terms and privacy policies.

03

Data and the purpose of processing it

We may collect the identity and contact data necessary to handle the request, the property data necessary to review it, and limited technical data to protect sharing links. This data is used to review the request, to communicate about it, to organise the documents made available through the platform, to protect the platform, and to fulfil the relevant regulatory obligations.

04

Data minimisation and protection

We ask for the minimum appropriate to the purpose, and we restrict the team’s access to what its task requires. Title deeds, identity documents and powers of attorney are not made available through client sharing links. In addition, link tokens are stored as unreadable fingerprints (hashes), and viewing links expire at the set time and can be revoked immediately.

05

Sharing of data and documents

We do not share data or attachments outside the scope of the transaction except on a legal basis, with appropriate consent, or out of operational necessity. When documents relating to a property are shared with an invited party, the sharing is limited to the photographs, plans or sketches made available through the link, and does not include the owner’s data, the title deed, the identity document or the power of attorney.

06

Retention and destruction

We retain data and documents for the period necessary to achieve the operational or regulatory purpose, and then review them for destruction or masking in accordance with our internal procedures. Grants of access to shared files end automatically when the link expires or when it is revoked.

07

Rights of the data subject

The data subject may request to be informed about the processing, to access their data, to obtain it and to correct it, to request its destruction where the regulatory conditions apply, and to withdraw consent in cases where consent is the basis for the processing. Requests are submitted through the company’s official contact channel.

08

Updates

We may update this policy when the platform’s services or the protection requirements change. The version in force is published on this page together with the date of the last update.

Regulatory references

This policy is based on the principles of the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) and the guidance of the Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA) on privacy notices and the rights of data subjects.
